# ZigSlice · 闪切

**让人和 AI 在可信的工作空间中，把想法变成可验证的成果。**

闪切希望让复杂工作变得清晰、可协作、可追溯。人提出目标、掌握关键决定；AI 帮助理解上下文、制定步骤并推进工作；领域系统负责规则、权限、执行与结果验证。无论是开发软件、协调制造流程，还是处理需要谨慎保护的专业材料，工作都应在明确的边界内持续推进，并留下能够核对的证据。

我们的方向是构建**一个统一产品和可扩展的领域能力体系**：Core 提供身份、团队、工作空间、项目、授权与工作流运行机制；每个 **Slice** 定义一个领域的工作流、技能、工具和业务数据。新的领域可以加入同一工作体验，同时保有自己的规则与数据归属。

我们坚持几个原则：

- **以目标组织工作。** 从人的意图出发，将过程拆成可执行、可恢复、可验收的步骤。
- **让 AI 在规则内行动。** AI 可以理解和编排任务；关键动作由领域规则、权限检查及必要的人工确认约束。
- **让数据留在合适的位置。** 个人设备、团队仓库或领域服务按实际需要持有内容；连接和协作不意味着默认集中所有数据。
- **用证据说明完成。** 计划、执行、发布和验收是不同阶段；结果应能被人查看、验证和追溯。

这一愿景正在由多个独立仓库共同推进：[Core](https://github.com/zigslice/zs_core) 提供平台机制；[开发 Slice](https://github.com/zigslice/zs_x)、[制造 Slice](https://github.com/zigslice/zs_maf) 与[小律 Slice](https://github.com/zigslice/zs_law) 探索不同领域；[Agent](https://github.com/zigslice/zs_agent)、[设计契约](https://github.com/zigslice/zs_design) 和[本地数据保护](https://github.com/zigslice/zs_data) 提供相关支撑。各方向的实现和交付状态以对应仓库的代码与文档为准。

## Vision

**Help people and AI turn ideas into verifiable outcomes in a trusted workspace.**

ZigSlice aims to make complex work clear, collaborative, and traceable. People set goals and retain control of consequential decisions. AI helps understand context, plan steps, and move work forward. Domain systems enforce rules, permissions, execution, and verification. Whether the work involves software, manufacturing, or sensitive professional materials, it should progress within explicit boundaries and leave evidence that people can inspect.

We are building **one unified product with extensible domain capabilities**. Core provides identity, teams, workspaces, projects, authorization, and workflow execution. Each **Slice** defines the workflows, skills, tools, and data of a domain. New domains can join the same work experience while retaining their own rules and data ownership.

Our principles are:

- **Organize around goals.** Turn human intent into steps that can be executed, resumed, and verified.
- **Keep AI within the rules.** AI can reason and coordinate; domain rules, permissions, and human approval govern consequential actions.
- **Keep data with its rightful owner.** Content belongs on personal devices, in team repositories, or in domain services as the work requires. Collaboration does not imply collecting everything centrally.
- **Show evidence of completion.** A plan, an execution, a release, and an accepted result are distinct stages. Outcomes should be inspectable and traceable.

Independent repositories are advancing this vision: [Core](https://github.com/zigslice/zs_core) provides the platform mechanisms; the [development](https://github.com/zigslice/zs_x), [manufacturing](https://github.com/zigslice/zs_maf), and [legal collaboration](https://github.com/zigslice/zs_law) Slices explore different domains; [Agent](https://github.com/zigslice/zs_agent), the [design contract](https://github.com/zigslice/zs_design), and [local data protection](https://github.com/zigslice/zs_data) provide supporting capabilities. See each repository's code and documentation for its current implementation and delivery status.
